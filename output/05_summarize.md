# Meeting Summary

This session was designed as a session to learn tools, reflect, share, unwind, and gather information/knowledge among participants.

## 1. Check-in & Emotional State

*   **Activity:** Participants were asked how they have been feeling this past week via a QR code/MCQ.
*   **Results:**
    *   The majority of participants reported feeling **tired and drained**.
    *   A very close majority also felt **anxious and unsettled**.
    *   A smaller minority felt okay and relieved, while some felt numb and disconnected.
*   **Overall Sentiment:** The session fostered a sense of community and validation ("making me feel like I'm not here alone").

## 2. Biggest Struggles Identified

Participants shared their current biggest struggles, which were grouped into several key themes:

*   **Workload & Hours:**
    *   Crazy working hours.
    *   Mental exhaustion due to working from home combined with long hours.
    *   Difficulty completing work requiring physical discussion; online discussions are ineffective.
*   **Work Environment & Compensation:**
    *   Loss of allowance (specifically mentioned: OERP - Overseas Allowance Remuneration Package).
    *   Financial uncertainty.
    *   Lack of transparency from bosses.
*   **Work-Life Balance & Logistics:**
    *   Difficulty balancing work life and family requirements.
    *   Challenges related to time zones and time management.
    *   Lack of work balance.
*   **Motivation:**
    *   Limited internal motivation.

## 3. Coping Mechanisms & Discussion Points

*   **Proposed Solution Discussion (Office Space):**
    *   The idea of moving to the KLCC office was discussed as a potential solution for working from home difficulties.
    *   **Concern Raised:** The feasibility of the office hours, especially considering time zones (e.g., working until 10 PM Malaysia time), and the potential for loneliness if working late alone.

## Key Takeaways & Action Items

*   **Key Insight:** The primary shared struggles revolve around **excessive working hours, mental exhaustion, financial uncertainty (allowances), and managing time zone differences** while working remotely.
*   **Next Steps (Implied):** The discussion highlighted the need to explore solutions related to work-from-home arrangements, office accessibility, and managing work-life balance.
*   **Call to Action:** Participants were encouraged to continue sharing their experiences in the chat to keep the conversation active.
