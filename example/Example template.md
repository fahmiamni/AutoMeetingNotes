# Decisions

- Annual CPE evaluation replaces biannual cycle for 2026.
- CM in-country prepares CPE reports for 2026 and onwards.
- MyProcure system used for CPE evaluation with workflow assignment.
- Mandatory remarks required for low and high ratings in MyProcure.
- Presentation materials and support contacts shared with participants.

# Open questions

- Contract number naming conventions differ across IOs; no standardization decided.
- Summary reports from MyProcure not available; future improvement suggested.

# Meeting notes

### Meeting structure

- Asheiah explained that the CPE meeting combined the procurement and procurement onboarding sessions into a single meeting due to time constraints.

### Participants and roles

- Asheiah introduced the participants, including A.K., Che Abid, Puanita, Puansari, Dhamina, and Nuramirah, and stated their respective departments.

### Meeting agenda

- Asheiah stated that the first agenda item for the meeting is CPE reporting.

### CPE process changes

- Ahmad Khairul explained that the CPE evaluation frequency will change from biannual to annual, with a transition period requiring completion by end of August and reporting by September.
- Ahmad Khairul stated that ad hoc CPE assessments can still be conducted as needed, in addition to the new annual cycle.
- Ahmad Khairul announced the introduction of the myProcure system for CPE evaluations, replacing the manual process to improve structure and data integrity.
- Ahmad Khairul described the enhancement of the rating system from four-star to five-star, adding an operations element, and empowering in-country teams to draft and prepare CPE reports for faster submission.
- Siti Dhamina explained that the PGC team is leading contractor performance enhancement initiatives, starting with myProcure system improvements, followed by a comprehensive review and revision of the CPE framework and questionnaires, pending AE's approval.
- Siti Dhamina explained that the myProcure system introduced contract-based evaluations with a five-star rating system, enhanced operational elements, and 16 KPIs, including integrity as a demerit factor.
- Siti Dhamina described that myProcure reduces manual effort for CM evaluators and approvers, enables faster cycle completion, improves insights via standard KPIs, and strengthens governance with audit trails and escalation routes.
- Siti Dhamina explained that the myProcure scorecard now includes an approval stage, which was not present in the previous system.
- Siti Dhamina detailed the scorecard workflow in myProcure, including roles for initiator, evaluator, and approver, and the actions available at each stage of the evaluation process.

### CPE process requirements

- Siti Noor Asheiah explained that the annual CPE evaluation is required for contracts with a minimum one-year duration, suspended or terminated contracts, and contracts with incidents or issues, regardless of value.
- Siti Dhamina stated that in myProcure, CPE assessments are managed through a scorecard feature, where CM initiates the evaluation process.
- Siti Dhamina described the five evaluation elements in the scorecard, including capability, responsiveness, and demerit KPIs such as integrity and HSE incidents, which can significantly impact the final rating.
- Siti Dhamina outlined that contract nature classification and adjustable weightage are important in CPE evaluations, with specific rules for operational and project contracts.

### CPE reporting timeline

- Siti Noor Asheiah stated that the CPE notification was issued on July 9, and templates for reporting and myProcure were shared with participants.
- Siti Noor Asheiah stated that CPE evaluations for the 2026 cycle will be conducted annually from July to August, with submissions due by September 21, and ad hoc evaluations can be initiated for issues or incidents outside the cycle.

### CPE reporting requirements

- The CPE focal was required to conduct a kick-off with the CM and user teams to brief them on the process, timeline, and reporting requirements.
- Siti Noor Asheiah stated that a PowerPoint presentation is required for reporting to Petronas, as mandated by group governance, even if the evaluation is completed in the portal.

### CPE evaluation process

- Siti Dhamina explained that evaluators must include rationale or remarks for contracts rated one, two, or five stars in the myProcure system, as the system will not allow submission without justification.
- Nuramirah described that after scorecard approval, contract managers must syndicate the results with the VEC committee, and can either reevaluate or finalize the scorecard by entering syndication and concurrence dates and uploading supporting documents.
- Dhamina clarified that for international contracts, concurrence is provided by the head of procurement.
- Siti Dhamina clarified that the concurrence date is provided by the head of procurement IO, while the syndication date is approved by the PIKL team, and these are two separate dates.
- Ahmad Khairul explained that finalizing the rating is handled by business leadership in-country, and consolidation is presented to the head of procurement for verification.

### myProcure system workflow

- Nuramirah demonstrated the process for contract managers to initiate and complete the scorecard in the myProcure system, including downloading and filling the template, assigning evaluators, and validating contract information.
- Nuramirah explained that contract nature selection in the template determines the KPIs for evaluators, and provided guidance on handling missing supplier names in the system.
- Nuramirah explained that after assigning evaluators to each scorecard in myProcure, the system will send email notifications to the evaluators and CC the initiator.
- Nuramirah demonstrated how contract managers can verify assigned evaluators and trigger the scorecard initiation process in myProcure, allowing them to track the status of each contract evaluation.
- Nuramirah explained that the approval process for the scorecard allows for either offline or online approval, with evidence required for offline approvals.
- Nuramirah demonstrated that approvers, such as Haris, can either approve or return the scorecard with remarks, and all related parties receive email notifications at each step.

### CPE evaluation requirements

- Siti Dhamina emphasized that evaluators must provide remarks for low-rated contracts, including background on incidents leading to one or two star ratings.

### Support and troubleshooting

- Nuramirah offered support for login or account issues and stated participants can contact them for assistance with system access or missing supplier names.
- Siti Noor Asheiah stated that any issues with the myProcure system should be communicated with the technology or digital teams, and CPE-related matters should be addressed with the CPE focal.

### myProcure system usage

- Nuramirah explained that users can add contracts in the system either manually or by uploading an Excel file, and both methods are supported for entering contract data.
- Nuramirah clarified that if a required contract nature is missing from the system, users can request the PGC team to add it to myProcure.
- Nuramirah and Dhamina confirmed that the system supports contracts with combined material and service scopes, and users must select contract nature and supplier from predefined lists, not free text.
- Sariana clarified that international operations will use an Excel template to initiate contract listings for CPE evaluation in myProcure, while domestic operations will extract contract listings from STMPEGT, with separate columns for contract numbers and titles.

### Data standardization

- Sariana suggested that teams within international operations should standardize the contract number naming convention for consistent use in templates.

### myProcure evaluation workflow

- Nuramirah explained that evaluators receive an email with a two-factor authentication code to access the myProcure scorecard, and must provide mandatory remarks for ratings of one, two, or five stars.
- Nuramirah clarified that the demerit rating will override the scorecard rating if selected, and evaluators can use the toggle scoring feature to understand how the final score is calculated.
- Nuramirah demonstrated that evaluators can reassign the evaluation to another person and send the scorecard for approval by specifying the approver's name and email with remarks.

### Reporting process improvements

- Ainul suggested extracting future summary reports directly from the system instead of preparing separate PowerPoint presentations.
